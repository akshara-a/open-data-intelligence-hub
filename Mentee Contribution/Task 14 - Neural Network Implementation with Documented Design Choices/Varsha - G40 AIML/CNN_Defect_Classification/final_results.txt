------------------------------------------

defective.

135 defective images were incorrectly classified as
non-defective.

The model achieved high precision of 94.36%, meaning that
most images predicted as defective were actually defective.

The recall for the defective class was 70.20%. Recall is
important because the system should detect as many defective
products as possible. A defective product incorrectly
classified as non-defective may pass the quality inspection.

CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------

The developed Convolutional Neural Network successfully
classifies casting product images into non-defective and
defective categories.

The model achieved a test accuracy of 78.46%, precision of
94.36%, and recall of 70.20%.

The high precision indicates that the model is reliable when
it predicts an image as defective. However, the confusion
matrix shows that 135 defective images were classified as
non-defective. Therefore, improving defective-class recall
would be an important area for future improvement.

Overall, the CNN demonstrates that automated image-based
quality inspection can be used to identify defective casting
products.
