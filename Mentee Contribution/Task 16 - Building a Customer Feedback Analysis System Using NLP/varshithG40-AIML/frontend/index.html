07 // BENCHMARK

Classical NLP (TF-IDF) vs. Modern Transformer (DistilBERT)

Examine how contextual multi-head self-attention handles nuanced linguistic complexities.

MODEL A • CLASSICAL PIPELINE

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression

  • Speed: Ultra-fast (< 2ms per inference).
  • Footprint: Extremely lightweight (~120KB serialized model).
  • N-Gram Scope: Captures local unigram & bigram frequencies.
  • Trade-off: Struggles with long-range inversions, sarcasm, or complex double-negatives.
MODEL B • DEEP LEARNING

DistilBERT Contextual Transformer

  • Architecture: 6 Transformer layers, 768 hidden dimension, 12 attention heads.
  • Self-Attention: Bidirectional contextual embedding of every word in relation to every other word.
  • Complex Negations: Handles nuanced sentence structures ("Not bad at all", "Hardly disappointing").
  • Trade-off: Requires PyTorch and GPU/CPU inference overhead (~40-80ms).
