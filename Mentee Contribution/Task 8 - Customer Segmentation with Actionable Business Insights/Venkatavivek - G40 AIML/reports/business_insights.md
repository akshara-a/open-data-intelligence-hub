# Business Insights

## Segment Analysis

After running the notebook, review `cluster_profile.csv` and name each cluster based on its measured behaviour.

For each segment document:

- Segment name
- Number of customers
- Average spending
- Purchase frequency
- Recency
- Average order value
- Average rating
- Discount usage
- Revenue contribution
- Recommended marketing action

## Recommended Interpretation Framework

### High-Value Loyal Customers
High frequency, high spending, recent purchases, and high order value.

**Action:** loyalty rewards, early product access, premium membership, and limited unnecessary discounting.

### New and Promising Customers
Recent engagement with low-to-medium purchase frequency and moderate spending.

**Action:** onboarding campaigns, popular-product recommendations, and second-purchase incentives.

### Discount-Driven Customers
High discount usage with moderate frequency and lower profit contribution.

**Action:** targeted promotions, limited-time discounts, and bundles.

### At-Risk Customers
Long recency combined with previously meaningful spending or engagement.

**Action:** re-engagement campaigns, personalized comeback offers, feedback requests, and new-product highlights.

### Low-Engagement Customers
Low frequency, spending, and website activity.

**Action:** low-cost campaigns and entry-level products; avoid excessive advertising spend.
