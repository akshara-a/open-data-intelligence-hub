# Business Recommendation Report

## Objective

Predict customers who are likely to purchase products and recommend suitable business strategies based on model predictions.

---

# High Purchase Likelihood Customers

Characteristics

- High Page Values
- More Product Related Pages
- Longer Session Duration
- Low Exit Rate

Recommendations

- Personalized product recommendations
- Premium membership offers
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Cart reminder emails

Expected Benefit

- Higher conversion rate
- Increased sales
- Better customer retention

---

# Medium Purchase Likelihood Customers

Characteristics

- Moderate browsing activity
- Medium engagement
- Occasional purchases

Recommendations

- Small discount coupons
- Product comparison emails
- Free shipping offers
- Personalized email campaigns

Expected Benefit

- Increased engagement
- Improved conversion

---

# Low Purchase Likelihood Customers

Characteristics

- High Exit Rate
- Low Page Values
- Short browsing sessions

Recommendations

- Awareness campaigns
- Website improvement
- Customer feedback collection
- Retargeting advertisements

Expected Benefit

- Better website engagement
- Reduced marketing cost

---

# Feature Importance Insights

Important Features

- PageValues
- ProductRelated
- ExitRates
- BounceRates
- Administrative Duration
- VisitorType

Business Interpretation

Customers spending more time on product pages and viewing more products have a higher purchase probability.

Customers with high exit rates are less likely to purchase.

---

# Final Recommendations

- Prioritize high-probability customers.
- Improve website engagement.
- Reduce exit rates.
- Personalize marketing campaigns.
- Send cart reminder notifications.
- Offer discounts only to medium-probability customers.
- Improve product recommendations using browsing history.

---

# Conclusion

The optimized Random Forest model provides accurate purchase predictions and helps businesses make data-driven marketing decisions while improving customer conversion and reducing unnecessary marketing expenditure.
